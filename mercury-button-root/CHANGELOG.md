## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-24

### Added
- Liquid-metal glass button with constant-volume liquid
- Device-orientation tilt with spring slosh
- Press surge toward the touch point, splash and bead-off
- Full and lite render tiers with automatic downgrade
- Size modifiers (`--sm`, `--lg`) and gold variant
- `MercuryButton.init`, `setQuality`, `setMouseTilt`
- TypeScript declarations
